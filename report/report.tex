\title{Categorical Encoding and Threshold Choice for BRFSS Classification}
\input{authors.tex}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We classify self-reported myocardial infarction or coronary heart disease in
an imbalanced BRFSS-derived survey dataset. Ten NumPy linear and logistic
configurations compare imputation, missing indicators, categorical encoding
and regularization on separate development and tuning partitions.
At a common threshold, categorical encoding increases tuning F1 by
\CategoryGain{}; a development-only fold check finds positive differences
in all three folds. The frozen regularized logistic model obtains holdout
F1 \HoldFone{}, accuracy \HoldAccuracy{} and recall \HoldRecall{}.
Threshold choice substantially changes this tradeoff. Optimization remains
budget-limited, while survey structure and self-report prevent interpreting
these results as prospective clinical validation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Data and experimental design}
The target \texttt{\_MICHD} concerns reported history of myocardial infarction
or coronary heart disease, rather than verified future disease onset.
The supplied data contain \NTrain{} labeled rows and \NTest{} test rows with
\NFeatures{} features. There are \NPositive{} positives (\Prevalence{}\%).
Predicting every row negative therefore obtains 0.9117 accuracy,
but no true positives, zero recall and zero F1.

We report unweighted sample-level classification. BRFSS uses telephone
sampling, design weights and raking~\cite{overview}; neither our loss nor our
evaluation uses those weights as observation weights. Sample prevalence and
performance are consequently not design-adjusted population estimates.
Raw missingness is \TrainMissing{}\% in training and \TestMissing{}\% in test.
Schemas and label/test IDs align. No exact duplicate rows were found across
all raw columns, including survey keys; this does not establish
respondent-level independence.

Seed \Seed{} defines a stratified split of \NDevelopment{} development,
\NTuning{} tuning and \NHoldout{} holdout rows. Imputation, scaling, categories,
references and indicator inclusion use only the fitting partition, respecting
the training-fold boundary~\cite{leakage}.
The original holdout was opened after selection. This review checks its
frozen scores; no new model or threshold is selected using holdout labels.

F1 is our retained minority-class development objective.
Written AIcrowd rules specify accuracy~\cite{rules}; an observed leaderboard
displaying F1 first does not establish a current staff clarification.
Both metrics are reported; F1 is not claimed as the confirmed competition metric.

\section{Representation and learning}
\textbf{Preprocessing.}
We exclude \texttt{Id}, sequence number \texttt{SEQNO}, its duplicate
\texttt{\_PSU}, and raw date \texttt{IDATE}. We also exclude \texttt{HAREHAB1},
a rehabilitation question with heart-attack-dependent availability~\cite{codebook}.
All \ProxyObserved{} nonmissing training entries belong to positives.
This specific proxy check is not an exhaustive audit of every survey module.

Numeric columns receive fitted mean or median fill and fitted mean/std scaling;
fully observed constants and all-missing columns are removed.
Optional indicators encode missingness observed during fitting.
The categorical variant reference-encodes integer columns with at most twelve
observed fitting levels. The most frequent level is the reference, with ties
favoring the lowest value; unseen levels map to the reference.
This increases flexibility and dimensionality without imposing ordinal spacing.
Higher-cardinality nominal codes can still remain numeric.
We do not claim complete semantic cleaning: \texttt{PHYSHLTH}=88, for example,
means zero unhealthy days~\cite{codebook}, so universal sentinel replacement
is unjustified.

\textbf{Models.}
The six required methods retain the specified signatures, one-dimensional
weights and final-state data losses. Required ridge solves
$(X^\top X+2N\lambda I)w=X^\top y$, penalizing every supplied coordinate.
The optional linear baseline uses a Gram eigen pseudoinverse for redundancy;
positive ridge uses a float64 solve.
For optional logistic prediction, with $X$ of shape $N\times D$,
$y_i\in\{0,1\}$ and intercept $w_0$, we minimize
\begin{equation}
 J(w)=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N
 [\log(1+e^{x_i^\top w})-y_i x_i^\top w]
 +\lambda\sum_{j=1}^{D-1}w_j^2 .
\end{equation}
Its intercept convention is separate from the mandatory methods.
NumPy L-BFGS uses ten curvature pairs and Armijo backtracking.
The historical fit stores float32 designs and rounds optimization matvecs
to float32, with float64 weights and reductions.
That arithmetic is preserved for reproduction; a separate blocked float64
path passes finite-difference checks.
The frozen budget is \MaxIterations{} updates and the requested gradient
infinity norm is $10^{-5}$. The all-label final norm is \FinalGradient{},
so convergence is not established.

\textbf{Selection.}
Ten configurations compare processing and categorical-design penalties
$\lambda\in\{0,10^{-4},10^{-3},10^{-2},10^{-1}\}$.
Thresholds span 0.05--0.65 in steps of 0.01 on tuning predictions only;
equal metric values prefer higher thresholds.
Within 0.002 of maximum tuning F1, the recorded rule prefers fewer columns,
then stronger regularization, then higher F1. It selects
$\lambda=\SelectedLambda{}$, threshold \SelectedThreshold{} and
\SelectedDimension{} columns. This tolerance is a deliberate preference,
not evidence of superior generalization.

\section{Results and interpretation}
Table~\ref{tab:models} uses each model's tuning-selected threshold.
The unregularized categorical candidate reaches \UnregularizedFone{} tuning F1,
slightly above the selected model's \TuningFone{}, within the stated tolerance.
Table~\ref{tab:ablation} instead fixes the threshold at 0.20.
Categorical coding gives the largest representation gain; imputation and
indicator changes have much smaller effects. These are tuning comparisons,
not causal or statistically significant improvements.
A subsequent numeric-versus-categorical check on three shared development-only
folds, with $\lambda=10^{-3}$ and the same budget and threshold, gives positive
F1 differences from \CategoryRobustMin{} to \CategoryRobustMax{}.
This supports repeatability on reused data, not independent evaluation of
the full search. Increasing L2 to $10^{-2}$ lowers tuned F1 to \StrongLtwoFone{}.

On the frozen holdout, F1 is \HoldFone{}, accuracy \HoldAccuracy{},
balanced accuracy \HoldBalanced{}, precision \HoldPrecision{} and recall
\HoldRecall{}. Counts are TP=\HoldTP{}, FP=\HoldFP{}, FN=\HoldFN{} and

The tuning-selected threshold adds \ThresholdFoneGain{} holdout F1 while
losing \ThresholdAccuracyLoss{} accuracy.
An accuracy-selected tuning threshold of \AccuracyTunedThreshold{} instead
gives accuracy \AccuracyTunedScore{} and F1 \AccuracyTunedFone{}.
No alternative replaces the verified CSV.

Figure~\ref{fig:threshold} illustrates the objective tradeoff.
For calibrated conditional probabilities, the expected-F1-optimal threshold
equals half the optimal F1~\cite{lipton}. Calibration is not established here,
so that special result neither determines nor validates our empirical threshold.
Finite-sample selection can itself overfit~\cite{cawley}.
The fixed configuration gives three-fold non-holdout F1
\CVMean{} $\pm$ \CVStd{} (sample SD, not a standard error).
Each fold refits processing and calibrates its threshold inside outer training,
but family selection is not nested.
A conditional IID-row bootstrap interval for holdout F1 is
[\HoldCILower{}, \HoldCIUpper{}]; it omits survey design and selection variability.

\section{Limitations and reproducibility}
High missingness, incomplete coding semantics, survey structure and self-report
limit generalization. False negatives remain \HoldFNRPercent{}\% of positives,
and increased recall introduces many false positives.
Training F1 \TrainingFone{} versus tuning \TuningFone{} does not eliminate
selection bias. A development-only float64 diagnostic reduced objective and
gradient norm without a consistent F1 gain; the frozen model is retained.
Clinical validity, population fairness and causal interpretation are unestablished.

After selection, all-label fitting discovers additional categories, giving
\FinalDimension{} final columns. Complete fitted state accompanies the weights.
From the repository root, use \texttt{python run.py} with
\texttt{--data-dir ../dataset}, \texttt{--output new.csv} and \texttt{--seed 433}.
Test inputs and saved state suffice. The original CSV is reproduced exactly
in Python 3.12.14 / NumPy 2.5.3 with four OpenBLAS threads, including a separate
legacy refit; cross-environment bitwise identity is unverified.
Students must supply the final public commit permalink after review.
AI-assisted code and writing require student verification and course-specific
disclosure; staff authorization remains unverified. No competition acceptance
or external score is claimed.
